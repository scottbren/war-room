## T8: navmesh (risk prototype)
- Baker: `tools/src/navGeometry.ts` builds the triangle soup (flat floor slabs with holes for openings, wall and solid-cover blocks with a downward lid, closed windows, vertical links as off-mesh connections); `tools/src/bake-navmesh.ts` bakes one solo navmesh to `packages/content/generated/charon.navmesh.bin` (about 430 KB, about 2 s). A test checks the committed binary equals a fresh bake.
- **Deviation from the task text (agent 0.35 m, cell 0.2 m):** the baker uses cell 0.1 m and agent radius 0.3 m. With 0.2 m cells and 0.35 m radius every 1.0 and 1.2 m doorway in the map erodes shut (the wall faces already take a voxel on each side). Doors keep a clear width of door width minus 0.6 m.
- Cover 0.4 m high or lower (pool basins, kerbs) is stepped over; concealment (hedges, trees) is walkable; walls and cover are capped 0.2 m under the floor above so ground-floor walls cannot leak into the upper layer.

Floors that share a height (grounds/ground, hill/upper) are told apart by which floor has a room under the point.
- **createSim stays synchronous.** recast needs an async `init()`, so the caller (worker or test) awaits `createNav` and will pass the ready `Nav` into `createSim` as an option when T10 wires movement to it. Not wired yet; movement is still straight-line.
- Vertical links are plain off-mesh connections: `restrictedTo` (principal and family only) is not enforced by the mesh; the sim will enforce it.

## T9: line of sight (risk prototype)
- `sim/src/los.ts`: `createLos(map)` returns `{ sight(from, to, stance), setDoor, setShutter, cellValue }`; also `effectiveRange(rangeCm, concealed)` (concealment halves detection range, integer). Per-floor grids of 0.5 m cells; ray tracing is an integer Amanatides-Woo DDA on centimetre coordinates (no division in the loop).
- Cell values: 0 open, 1 low cover (0.41 to 1.59 m: hides a crouched target, not a standing one), 2 full block (walls, closed or locked doors, closed shutters, cover 1.6 m or taller). Cover 0.4 m or lower is ignored (same as the navmesh step-over rule). Concealment is a separate flag: never blocks, marks the target concealed if it stands in it or the ray passes through it.
- Walls rasterize along their centre line (so a thin wall costs one cell, not two); doors and shuttered windows are dynamic overlays on the wall gap. A ray that passes exactly through a cell corner needs both side cells open (no squeezing between two blocked cells). A segment that ends exactly on a corner is handled (found by a test).
- Vertical sight: only through an upper-floor opening whose `below` is the other floor, and only to a point under the opening. The observer is projected to the nearest point of the void; leg 1 (on the upper floor, low cover ignored: a balustrade is looked over) must be clear, leg 2 is traced on the lower floor with normal rules. Works both ways.
- Diagonal walls are refused (the map has none; they would otherwise fill their whole bounding box).
- Known limits: sight does not yet use elevation (a roof observer cannot look down through anything), and positions exactly on a wall line are ambiguous by design (units are never placed inside walls).
- Not wired into the pipeline yet (perception system comes later); door and shutter changes will call `setDoor` and `setShutter` from the sim systems.

## T10: client and worker bridge
- **Nav is passed into `createSim` as an optional `nav` (not saved state).** With a nav, `move` computes a route (`findPath`) and the unit follows the waypoints; an unreachable target is rejected with a `commandRejected` event ("no route to target"). Without one, movement stays the old straight line (so existing tests are unchanged). `arrived` fires once, when the whole route ends.
- Floor changes along a route (stairs, lift, tunnel) happen as a pop on arrival at the link end: the unit walks to the link on the source floor, then appears on the target floor. Smooth stair animation is later polish.
- Worker protocol (`client/src/protocol.ts`): page to worker `init {seed}` and `moveTo {entity, candidates[]}`; worker to page `ready {snapshot}`, `tick {snapshot, events}`, `error {message}`. A click is sent as one candidate per visible floor (nearest along the camera ray first); the worker uses the first one the navmesh calls walkable, so clicking where a roof is hidden or absent falls through to the floor below.
- The worker steps at 10 Hz with `setInterval`; commands are enqueued with the last completed tick so they apply on the next step. The page keeps the last two snapshots and interpolates over the measured tick interval (clamped to 50-400 ms), jumping when a unit is new or changes floor.
- Engine: WebGPU when `WebGPUEngine.IsSupportedAsync` and init succeeds, otherwise WebGL2; `?gl=webgl2` forces the fallback. Overlay shows backend, fps, tick.
- Debug scenario: `scenario.ts` adds one operator (`op.alpha.1`) on the helipad (48, 68). Real spawns come with mission data.
- Verified in Chromium through Playwright: the operator walks helipad to study on both WebGPU and WebGL2, reaching (54, 37) exactly.

## T11: camera effects and performance
- **Effects are full-screen `PostProcess`es with the shader written twice** (GLSL in `ShaderStore.ShadersStore`, WGSL in `ShadersStoreWGSL`, same name) and `shaderLanguage` picked from the backend. Night vision, grain, fisheye and thermal all compile and render on WebGPU and WebGL2 (`client/src/fx/effects.ts`).
- **Thermal: choose approach (b), swapping materials in place, as the default; keep (a) for picture-in-picture.** Measured on the GTX 1060: (b) costs about +0.1 ms, (a) (a second full render of the scene with replacement materials) about +0.7 to +0.9 ms. (b) is cheaper because no extra pass is drawn; (a) leaves the main view untouched, so it is the one to use if a thermal image must be shown while the normal view stays visible (a helmet cam in thermal). The heat value per mesh comes from its material key (`fx/heat.ts`: people 1.0, concealment 0.3, water 0.05-0.1, slab 0.3, etc.), snapped to five buckets so five shared materials cover every mesh.
- **Helmet cams: 320x180 render targets, rotate the refresh** (one camera per frame). Every-frame costs +1.7 ms (WebGL2) and +3.1 ms (WebGPU); rotating costs +1.0 ms on both. Shown as a 2x2 overlay of planes parented to the main camera in rendering group 1.
- Measurement method and numbers: `docs/perf-notes.md`.

Default behavior is unchanged. Shots are not drawn yet. One full-suite run timed out the T12 replay test at 5.1 s under load; it passes alone and on rerun. — Scott

## 2026-11-06: Combat C2 built: cover and suppression (no contract change)
Second step of `docs/combat-spec.md`. No public type changed; new state is internal and saved only while present, so quiet runs hash as before (sim regression pin and T12 replay unchanged).
- **Cover:** a target's hit chance is multiplied by the best cover within 80 cm of it on the shooter's side: heavy x50%, light x75%. Concealment gives no penalty. Cover `facing` is treated as all-round for now. The map schema has no "exposed" cover type, so the spec's x125% for exposed is not built. Standing inside a cover rectangle counts.
- **Moving target** x75%. **Suppressed shooter** x60% at 50+, x40% at 80+.
- **Suppression** (`world.suppression`, saved as `suppression: [{id, v}]` only while above zero): a shot adds 25 to its target on a hit and 10 on a miss, and 10 to every other unit of the target's side within 2 m of the shot's path. Capped at 100, decays 1 per tick, cleared when a unit goes down. A unit at 80+ is pinned: movement skips it until it recovers. Restore rejects values outside 1 to 100, unknown or downed units and duplicates.
- **Not done:** darkness, defender cover-seeking (C3), operator behavior (C4), morale (C5). Pinned units do not yet change stance or behavior beyond holding still.
- **Tests:** `cover.test.ts` (hit rates behind heavy and light cover, cover on the wrong side or too far, moving target, suppression amounts, decay and cap, shooter penalty, pinned movement, downed unit, restore mid-fight and bad saves). Two slow tests (T12 replay in Node, the new hit-rate statistics) got an explicit 30 s timeout, because they took over the 5 s default when the whole suite runs in parallel; assertions are unchanged. — Scott

## 2026-11-06: Combat C3 built: defender combat behavior (no contract change)
Third step of `docs/combat-spec.md`. No public type changed; two optional fields on the internal defender mind (`cover`, `fallback`), saved only while set, so quiet runs hash as before (sim regression pin and T12 replay unchanged).
- **Engage first, then react.** An alerted defender fires from where it stands. The first time it is under fire (suppression above zero) in an alert, it reacts once:
  - **Fall back:** if it currently sees more living operators than it has alerted allies within 15 m (itself included) and the principal is alive, it walks toward the principal and stops 2 m short. If the principal is on another floor it needs a navmesh route.
  - **Take cover:** otherwise it picks the best cover spot within 10 m: a point 50 cm outside a heavy or light cover rectangle where the cover protects against the alert spot (same rule as the hit-chance cover). Heavy before light, then nearest, skipping a spot within 1 m of one another defender has taken. If it is already covered, or nothing qualifies, it stays and records its own place so it does not decide again.
- **No shooting on the run.** A defender walking to cover or falling back does not stop to shoot, unless an operator is within 10 m. Once it stops it fires as before. A new sighting keeps a running defender running (it only turns to face the spot).
- **Calming down** clears `cover` and `fallback`. Restore rejects either on a defender that is not alerted.
- **Facing known threats** needed no new code: a defender already faces its alert spot and, when it fires, its target.
- **Not done:** peeking in and out of cover, leaving cover when the threat moves, wounded retreat (C5), calling for help beyond the existing radio, reacting to being flanked. Only defenders do this; operators come in C4. — Scott

## 2026-11-06: Combat C4 built: operator behavior and rules of engagement (no contract change)
Fourth step of `docs/combat-spec.md`. No public type changed; new internal state (`roe`, `opCover`) is saved only when set, so quiet runs hash as before.
- **Rules of engagement** (`world.roe`): `hold` never fires, `return` (the default, and what operators did since C1) fires only at defenders that are alerted or already firing, `free` fires at any defender in view. Saved only when not `return`.
- **Not wired to plans yet.** `issuePlan` is still rejected ("not implemented until week 2"), and the `Plan` type is unchanged. When plans are executed, the ROE should come from `rules` strings on tasks; the vocabulary proposed is `roe:hold`, `roe:return`, `roe:free`. For now ROE can only be set by editing saved state (tests do this), so there is no way to change it in play.
- **Cover for operators** (`systems/operators.ts`, in the `ai` slot, step 2 of the pipeline; it acts on the previous tick's sight and suppression): a standing operator who is suppressed while a defender is in view picks the best cover near him against the closest such defender, once per fight, using the same rule as defenders (now `systems/cover.ts`, shared). An operator with a move order keeps to it. A move command cancels his cover choice. It ends when he has no suppression and no defender in view, or when he goes down.
- **No shooting on the run**, as for defenders: a running operator holds fire unless a defender is within 10 m.
- **Technical design:** step 2 note updated. The pipeline test names the step `operatorSystem` now.
- **Not done:** operators moving up or leaving cover on their own, covering each other, healing and carrying wounded (C5), anything team-level (stack, breach, clear). Operators with no orders and no fight stand where they are. — Scott
- **Pinned units can still retreat (fix to C2's rule).** A unit at suppression 80+ used to be frozen by movement, which also froze one running for cover, defeating the point. Now a unit that has chosen cover (operator `opCover`, defender `cover`) or is falling back keeps moving while pinned; only advancing is held. A test covers it (`operator-behavior.test.ts`). — Scott

## 2026-11-06: Combat C5a and C5b built: wounds, loyalty and morale (no contract change)
Fifth step of `docs/combat-spec.md` (the C5 proposal there). Scott approved C5a and C5b ("proceed, leave the decisions for later, just note them somewhere for now"); the surrender step C5c and the tuning thresholds are in `docs/open-decisions.md`. No public type changed; no new saved state except the defender mode value `broken`, so quiet runs hash as before.
- **Wounds (C5a):** derived from health alone. Health 31 to 60: walks at 75% speed, aims at 85%. Health 30 or less: 50% and 65%. Speed is the per-tick step times per-mille, truncated, at least 1 cm. At full health nothing changes. Applies to everyone.
- **Loyalty (C5b):** a defender's spawn marker may carry `guard:<hired|veteran|inner-circle>`. The sim reads it strictly (`loyalty.ts`); an unknown value, or the tag on anyone but a defender, fails `createSim` naming the marker. An untagged defender is a veteran, so nothing existing changes. It is read from the map (`SightEnv.loyalty`), not saved. Deviation from the proposal: the testbed gets no guard tags; tests tag a copy of the map in memory. The map validator does not check guard tags yet (CHARON's tags are valid and `createSim` checks them).
- **Morale:** only an alerted hired guard breaks: at suppression 60, at health 30 or less, or when under fire with no other alerted defender within 15 m. Breaking sets the new defender mode `broken`: the guard drops any cover or fallback choice, stops shooting (except at point-blank), and runs for the reachable point (eight directions at 25, 15 and 8 m) farthest from the threat's last known spot, staying put if none is reachable. A broken guard ignores sightings, radio calls and camera reports, makes none, and does not recover in this slice. Operators on "return fire" do not shoot at it (it is neither alerted nor firing). Restore rejects a broken mind that has a pending report, search time, cover spot or fallback.
- **Effect on CHARON:** its 6 hired guards will now break in a real fight; its 12 veterans never do. Replay hashes and the sim regression pin did not move.
- **Not done:** surrender and prisoners (C5c, open), recovery of a broken guard, veteran counterattack, inner-circle "rush the principal" (the map has no inner-circle markers yet), operator morale. — Scott
- **Review fixes applied to C5b:** a broken guard is not frozen by the 80+ pin (it is treated as retreating, like going to cover); a guard that was firing when it broke starts running at once; a fleeing guard never shoots, and one with nowhere to run (no motion) fires only at point-blank; the "alone under fire" test uses who was alerted at the start of the tick, so one guard breaking does not make the next look alone. Known and left: a guard whose threat is on another floor breaks and stays put; restore does not check that a broken mind belongs to a hired guard. — Scott
